# Selective figure specialist review

Use the matching catalogue's figure-specific review during normal authoring.
Consider a bounded figure review when it carries the main explanation, combines
difficult relationships, or retains a material misunderstanding after revision.
Name the uncertainty another reviewer should resolve. Skip delegation when the
author can resolve it directly from the evidence.
Do not launch an agent for every figure or repeat review merely to add polish.
The specialist advises; the document author owns edits and consistency.

## Review packet

Give the reviewer:

- Reader profile, the figure's question, and the task the reader must perform.
- Figure source with stable IDs, adjacent main-path prose, and authored details.
- Relevant source excerpts and their provenance, including uncertainty or gaps.
- Source formulas, symbol definitions, units, and assumptions when math is material.
- Matching catalogue reference and the shared visual-language reference.
- Current rendered images at desktop and narrow widths, when available.
- Shared terminology, related figure relationships, and the permitted review scope.

Withhold expected answers and previous findings for an independent first pass.
Do not send unrelated repository history. A source-aware specialist is not the
source-blind reader required by the staged review.

## Reusable subagent brief

> Review only the supplied figure and its immediate context. Do not edit or
> delegate. Read the source evidence before assessing the explanation. Apply the
> matching catalogue checks and shared visual language. Determine whether this
> representation answers the reader's question; a simpler representation is valid.
> Check the important relationships, conditions, terminology, and useful depth.
> Check notation, units, assumptions, and a boundary case when math is material.
> Inspect supplied renders for misleading geometry and legibility; without them,
> mark visual checks unperformed. Do not infer successful interaction from an image.
> Return material findings with target ID, likely wrong interpretation, source
> or rendered evidence, and the smallest correction. Identify correct non-obvious
> features that corrections must preserve. Separate defects from optional ideas.
> Do not invent missing facts, require decoration, or meet a finding quota.
> Stop after one pass and state checks not performed.

## Integration

Check each finding against evidence. Reject unsupported criticism with a reason.
Apply supported corrections under one writer; keep existing IDs and source rules.
Recheck shared terms, repeated content, cross-figure relationships, and page flow.
Preserve neutral styling unless emphasis or a supported group improves understanding.
Rebuild and inspect changed figures; rerun affected reader tasks. Do not restart
all reviews for small edits. Keep this review record outside the published document.
